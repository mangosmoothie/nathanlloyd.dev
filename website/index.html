No. 2

Social Media Manager

A calendar goes in. On-brand posts come out, on schedule.

Every calendar event becomes a post drafted in a reusable Personality: a named voice and content strategy. Posts wait in a review queue, then publish to LinkedIn, X and Facebook. Sync rules handle reschedules and cancellations, other automations can file posts through a token-authenticated queue, and every publish attempt is recorded. It runs as a private deployment.
