Adventure Guide

An invite-only meeting planner for Cub Scout packs. A leader picks an Adventure and a time limit; AI drafts activities and a prep list that fit; the published agenda lands on the pack calendar for everyone to follow.

  • Structured generation with content guardrails, section locks and per-leader quotas
  • Invite-only accounts with Leader and Member roles
  • Next.js on serverless AWS, invitations through Amazon SES

Social Media Manager

Turns a calendar into a publishing schedule. Every event becomes a post drafted in a reusable brand voice, waits in a review queue, then publishes to LinkedIn, X and Facebook on time.

  • Personalities: reusable voice and content strategy for every post
  • Calendar sync that handles reschedules, edits and cancellations
  • Token-authenticated queue so other automations can file posts
  • Dry-run mode and an immutable audit trail of every publish attempt
